What the add-in does

Legislative Report Router helps users find and review legislative filings, assign division recipients, prepare report drafts, and publish confirmed Teams posts and division emails. Its Teams Work Queue records response versions, assigned reviews and approvals, fiscal information, and completed submission records in the organization's SharePoint lists. Users must review content, recipients, deadlines and document versions before taking action. The queue does not automatically submit documents to LSA or detect edits to linked files. Your organization's procedures remain the authority for reviews, approvals and submissions.
