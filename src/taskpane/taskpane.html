⚙️ Setup (one-time, guided)

Everything here is saved to your account — walk through it once and the add-in reconnects automatically on every email, on every device.

Step 1 of 6

1 · Sign in & choose your legislature

One popup, one time. The add-in only ever acts as you.

2 · Where should your lists live?

The add-in keeps three lists: your division routes (channels, tags, and each division's contact emails), the audit trail, and the bill tracker. Pick a site your review team can access — or, if it's just you, use your OneDrive.

3 · Add each division — its channel and its tag

One or many channels: add a division again with a second channel and bills post to both. Every division should have a tag — that's what makes the right people get notified. No tag yet? Create one right here.

4 · Put people on divisions

Or paste many at once (Name, Email, Division — straight from Excel; "MVD/TDD" assigns both). Download the fill-in template or :

5 · Let it write the Daily Bill Report

Runs while this pane is open (📌 pin the pane to keep it open all day). It drafts and assigns — you still review, publish, and press Send.
