Sign in with your work account. Microsoft may ask you to sign in again when your session expires.

2 · Where should your lists live?

The add-in keeps three lists: your division routes (channels, tags, and each division's contact emails), the audit trail, and the bill tracker. Choose a SharePoint team site that your reviewers can access. OneDrive personal storage cannot hold these lists.

3 · Set division destinations

Use the same legislative channel for multiple divisions. Each division’s notification tag should contain the coordinator(s) who receive the initial request. Keep broad email contacts in Step 4. Email-only routes can leave the channel and tag blank.

4 · Manage division email contacts

These addresses receive division emails. Add one person, paste rows from Excel, or edit the same SharePoint list in Teams. Adding an email here does not join a Team or change a notification tag.

Optional: also notify matched email contacts in Teams

Leave this off to keep coordinator notifications separate from the broad email roster. This older mode adds tag contacts to the email list and can directly mention matched email contacts. It does not provide two-way tag synchronization.

Only confirmed identity matches are used. Unmatched contacts remain email-only. Refresh adds contacts; it does not remove people or edit Team/tag membership.

Choose the division in the dropdown above. Paste Name, Email, Division rows to suggest matches, or enter emails here. A name suggestion only takes effect after you confirm it. People must already be visible in this Team's tags; add missing people in Teams first.

5 · Choose the daily report audience (optional)

When selected, the router combines all active division email contacts and removes duplicates. The field below adds extra recipients, such as leadership. Open Preview to review the combined audience before creating a report draft.
