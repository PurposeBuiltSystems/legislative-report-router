⚙️ Setup (one-time)

Everything here is saved to your account — set it up once and the add-in reconnects automatically on every email, on every device. You never repeat these steps.

First-time setup, three steps: ① pick your SharePoint site  ② create the lists  ③ add a route per division — team, channel, and tag are pickers, no IDs to paste.

Let the add-in write the Daily Bill Report

Publish can draft the Daily Bill Report email itself \u2014 built from the routed bills \u2014 addressed to your distribution list. It lands in your Drafts for review; nothing sends by itself.
